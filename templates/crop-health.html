About NASA NDVI

NDVI is an indicator derived from satellite reflectance data. It helps observe vegetation greenness and changes over time.


NASA MODIS MOD13Q1 provides vegetation indices at 250-metre spatial resolution using 16-day composite observations.


NDVI alone cannot diagnose crop diseases or identify the exact reason for vegetation stress. Cloud cover, observation quality, crop type and growth stage can affect interpretation.
